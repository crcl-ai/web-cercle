This architecture fosters a space for 100% human interaction, verified through native Apple Sign-In, eliminating bots, brands, and influencers to preserve the integrity of local recommendations.

For Brazil's creators, Capsule offers a distinct platform to reclaim the narrative of their cities, moving beyond algorithm-driven trends to define authentic local culture.
