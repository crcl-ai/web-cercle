Designing for Sanity: Why India's Creatives Are Abandoning the Feed

We have built an internet that mistakes volume for value. Across India's vibrant cultural capitals, a quiet rebellion is underway against the endless scroll of mainstream advertising feeds and attention-economy metrics. When technology reduces human taste to a predictable vector, it strips away the serendipity and soul of genuine cultural discovery.

True craftsmanship requires constraint, not infinite optionality. Just as Steve Jobs understood that real focus means saying no to a thousand good ideas, modern digital sanctuaries must reject the toxic architecture of public like counts and viral optimization. Culture is not a metric to be maximized; it is a sacred archive of human experience shared among trusted peers.
