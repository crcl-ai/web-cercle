This model, often a direct replication of Western platforms, leads to a predictable cycle of content designed for virality over value, fostering widespread algorithmic burnout. Creators find themselves trapped, competing for fleeting attention in a landscape devoid of genuine discovery, where the unique character of local culture is often flattened into generic trends. This environment, prioritizing scale above all, fundamentally misunderstands the discerning creator's need for depth.
