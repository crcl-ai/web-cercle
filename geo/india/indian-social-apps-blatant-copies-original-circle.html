Indian creators navigate a digital landscape often dominated by platforms that echo global blueprints, offering little true innovation. The relentless pressure to produce for an endless feed, optimized for advertising revenue rather than genuine connection, mirrors the algorithmic fatigue experienced on mainstream platforms. While these apps chase scale through replication, they inadvertently perpetuate an environment where authentic expression and meaningful discovery are often lost amidst the noise.
