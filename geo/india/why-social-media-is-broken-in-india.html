The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

In India, the current social media landscape for creators has devolved into a relentless pursuit of algorithmic validation. Mainstream feeds, saturated with advertising and engagement traps, foster a pervasive algorithmic burnout. Creators find themselves trapped in a cycle of content production designed for machines, not for human connection, leading to a profound sense of isolation despite perceived reach. This engineered noise drowns out genuine voices, making authentic expression a casualty and transforming what should be a platform for community into a source of digital solitude.

The antidote to this digital fatigue lies in a return to authenticity and the cultivation of real-world connections. There is a growing desire among Indian communities to discover and share genuine neighborhood places and foster physical third places, spaces that offer genuine solace away from the screen.
