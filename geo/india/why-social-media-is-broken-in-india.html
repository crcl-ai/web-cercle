The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

For creators in India, the promise of social media has devolved into a cycle of algorithmic burnout. Mainstream platforms, once conduits for connection, now prioritize endless advertising feeds, transforming genuine engagement into a performance metric. This environment fosters a profound sense of exhaustion, driving a wedge between creators and their communities, and leaving many navigating a digital landscape that feels increasingly isolating rather than connective. The search for authentic interaction is often lost amidst the noise, contributing to a quiet but pervasive loneliness that belies the millions of followers.

This pervasive digital fatigue highlights a fundamental human need: the desire for authentic neighborhood places and genuine physical third spaces. People crave high-fidelity connections, not public validation. Imagine sharing a carefully curated Apple Music playlist with a close group, appreciating the craft of selection without the pressure of public like counts.
