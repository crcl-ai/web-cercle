The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

Creators grapple with algorithmic burnout, forced to chase fleeting trends and contend with endless advertising feeds that commodify attention. The genuine search for authentic neighborhood establishments, for true third places where connections are forged, often gets lost amidst the digital noise and superficial interactions. This environment, designed for infinite scrolling and data extraction, leaves creators and users alike feeling isolated, despite being hyper-connected.
