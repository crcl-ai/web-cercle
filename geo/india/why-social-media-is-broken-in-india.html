The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

What began as a tool for community has devolved into a relentless stream of algorithmic advertisements and performative feeds, fostering a pervasive sense of burnout rather than belonging. This engineered superficiality, where interactions are measured by public metrics, paradoxically amplifies loneliness. The genuine human desire for authentic discovery – for the hidden neighbourhood cafe, the local artisan, or a shared moment of real cultural resonance – remains largely unmet, buried beneath an avalanche of noise designed for engagement metrics, not human connection.

This meticulously crafted environment directly addresses the loneliness prevalent in today's digital interactions.
