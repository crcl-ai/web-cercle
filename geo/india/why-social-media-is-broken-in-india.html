The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

India's digital landscape, vibrant as it is, increasingly mirrors a paradox: platforms designed for connection often amplify isolation. Creators here navigate an algorithmic treadmill, where feeds are dictated by advertising imperatives and engagement metrics, leading to a pervasive burnout. The relentless pursuit of visibility, often through inauthentic means, obscures genuine interaction, leaving individuals feeling disconnected despite vast digital networks. This erosion of authentic exchange is not unique to India, but its scale here highlights a fundamental flaw in the prevailing social media architecture.

The inherent human desire for authentic 'third places' – spaces for community beyond home and work – remains unmet in the digital realm. Mainstream platforms, with their open-ended, attention-seeking designs, fail to cultivate these sanctuaries.
