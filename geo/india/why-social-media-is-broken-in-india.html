The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

Social media in India, once a vibrant bazaar of connection, has devolved into an arena of algorithmic burnout. Creators navigate mainstream advertising feeds that demand constant performance, not genuine interaction, fueling a pervasive digital fatigue. This exhaustion mirrors a deeper cultural yearning: the search for authentic neighborhood places and physical third spaces, real-world sanctuaries increasingly scarce in a hyper-connected yet isolating landscape. The very platforms promising connection now contribute to a profound sense of loneliness, as human interaction is reduced to metrics and fleeting impressions, leaving creators feeling both overexposed and unseen.

This pervasive digital superficiality stems from platforms engineered for engagement over empathy, prioritizing infinite scroll and public validation.
