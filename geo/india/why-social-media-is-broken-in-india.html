The Crisis of Algorithm Fatigue: Why Social Media is Broken in India

The digital landscape in India, while hyper-connected, often fosters a peculiar form of isolation. Creators navigate mainstream feeds dominated by algorithmic advertising and an endless scroll designed for consumption, not connection. This environment cultivates a distinct algorithmic burnout, where the pursuit of reach overshadows the value of genuine engagement. The promise of global access has inadvertently diluted the local, replacing authentic dialogue with a commodified attention economy that leaves many feeling overwhelmed and unseen, despite their digital presence.

This widespread digital fatigue has sparked a quiet resurgence: a desire for authentic neighborhood places, for physical third spaces where real-world discovery and interaction can flourish. Creators are seeking environments that prioritize depth over superficial virality, and curated experiences over algorithmically dictated feeds.
