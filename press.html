01 / Short description

Circle is an iOS app for saving songs, films, books, links, and places, making thoughtful collections, and discovering through people whose taste you trust.

02 / Product facts

  • The product name is Circle.
  • Circle is made for iOS.
  • People can save songs, films, books, links, places, photos, and notes.
  • A collection can contain up to 30 things.
  • Your circle means the people you follow.
  • The official account is @circle.
