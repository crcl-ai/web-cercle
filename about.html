What is Circle?

Circle is an iOS app for saving songs, films, books, links, and places, making thoughtful collections, and discovering through people whose taste you trust.

Published by CircleReviewed 13 October 2026

01 / Save things

A thing in Circle can be a song, film, book, link, place, photo, note, or another object that matters to you. The action is always Save.

You can add context so you remember who shared it, why it mattered, or who might enjoy it next.

02 / Make collections

A collection brings related things together around a place, trip, mood, idea, or person. Each collection has room for up to 30 things.

The boundary makes each collection deliberate: a short set someone can understand and explore.

03 / Discover through people

Your circle is the people you follow. Circle is designed so a recommendation carries the person and context behind it, instead of becoming an isolated item in a generic catalogue.

The homepage includes an interactive product preview showing saves, collections, activity, and local discovery.
